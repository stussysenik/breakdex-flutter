# Roadmap

This roadmap is the product steering view: what must be stabilized now, what expands the practice loop next, and what remains intentionally deferred.

## Now

| Lane | Goal | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review loop clarity | Make sessions calmer, more controllable, and easier to trust. | Active |
| Progress ergonomics | Keep parent-first navigation and immediate graph entry while reducing random analytics. | Active |
| Flow truthfulness | Ship honest move-first graph behavior before expanding to richer entities. | Active |
| Native media reliability | Keep import, album, and export paths stable on iOS. | Active |

## Next

| Lane | Goal | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combo and set graphing | Move beyond move-only nodes without lying about what is currently live. | Planned |
| Planning surfaces | Turn Lab and related sprint tools into genuinely useful preparation boards. | Planned |
| Stronger analytics | Grow calendar, heat-map, and retention signals around real coaching decisions. | Planned |
| Sync hardening | Tighten migration, conflict, and cloud consistency behavior. | Planned |

## Later

| Lane | Goal | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cross-platform parity | Keep iOS quality while validating broader device support. | Deferred |
| Research feedback loop | Feed scientific workbench findings back into product and scheduling decisions. | Ongoing |
| Coach and team workflows | Explore shared practice intelligence after the solo-athlete loop is stable. | Deferred |
