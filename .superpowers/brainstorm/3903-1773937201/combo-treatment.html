How step scrubbing works

A

Overlay Pill Strip (Recommended)

Tappable pills float center-screen over video. Active step is highlighted. Tap to switch step video. Minimal vertical space used — pills auto-hide after 3s like video controls.

B

Bottom Edge Step Dots

Numbered circles in the gradient scrim at bottom. Always visible alongside move name. More compact but takes space from the metadata overlay area.

C

Swipe Between Steps

Vertical swipe within the card to advance steps (like Instagram stories). No visible step UI — just a subtle page indicator. Most immersive but less discoverable.
