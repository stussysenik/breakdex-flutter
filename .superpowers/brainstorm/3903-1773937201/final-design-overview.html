Design Principles Applied

70/30 Rule

Video gets ~70% of screen. Rating strip gets ~30% importance — slim, functional, icon + interval only.

Autoplay Everywhere

Videos autoplay on visibility — both in the tab browse list and during review session. Tap to pause, never tap to play.

Overlay, Don't Stack

Move name, state pill, category all overlaid on video with gradient scrim. No separate metadata panel stealing pixels.

Steps Always Visible

Combo step timeline (numbered circles + connecting lines) is always visible at bottom of video. Tap to switch step. Rating is for the whole combo.
