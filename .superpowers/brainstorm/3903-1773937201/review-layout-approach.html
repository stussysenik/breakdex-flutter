Key Differences

Immersive Approach Gains

  • Video fills ~70% of screen (up from 42%)
  • Move name/state overlaid on video gradient — no wasted metadata panel
  • Progress shown as minimal dots instead of dashboard bar
  • Rating buttons condensed to a slim strip (~30% importance)
  • Autoplay on card visibility — tap to pause, not tap to play

Trade-offs

  • Less room for combo step line (needs separate treatment)
  • Overlaid text needs careful contrast on varied video content
  • Rating button text labels removed (icon + interval only)
